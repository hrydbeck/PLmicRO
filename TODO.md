# Journal Club — Project Plan

## Context

I am joining a journal club with researchers and a bioinformatician working in
clinical microbiology. This workspace is for organizing papers, notes, and
reading lists.

## Topics of Interest

1. **AI in clinical microbiology** — the primary focus
2. **Taxonomical profiling** — e.g. [nf-core/taxprofiler](https://nf-co.re/taxprofiler/1.0.1/)
3. **Microbiological surveillance** — e.g. [MIMOSA (GMS)](https://github.com/genomic-medicine-sweden/MIMOSA)

## My Personal Interests

- How to **compare and benchmark AI models** against each other
- Getting a broad understanding of **what AI is and what types of models exist**
  that could be useful in clinical microbiology

## What We Decided

- Start with a **review paper on AI in clinical microbiology**
- Build a **structured reading list** organized into ~6 sessions
- Create a **notes template** for each session (key concepts, discussion points, action items)

## Recommended Papers (to be put into reading_list.md)

### Session 1 — Intro to ML for Biologists
- Greener et al. (2022) — *A guide to machine learning for biologists*, Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology

### Session 2 — Benchmarking & Comparing AI Models
- Topçuoğlu et al. (2020) — *A Framework for Effective Application of Machine Learning to Microbiome-Based Classification Problems*, mBio

### Session 3 — AI in Clinical Microbiology (Reviews)
- Smith et al. (2023) — *Machine Learning and Deep Learning in Clinical Microbiology: A Review*, Clinical Microbiology Reviews
- Peiffer-Smadja et al. (2020) — *ML for clinical decision support in infectious diseases*, Clinical Microbiology and Infection

### Session 4 — MALDI-TOF + ML
- Weis et al. (2020) — *ML for microbial identification and AST on MALDI-TOF mass spectra*, Clinical Microbiology and Infection

### Session 5 — Deep Learning in Genomics
- Eraslan et al. (2019) — *Deep learning: new computational modelling techniques for genomics*, Nature Reviews Genetics
- Camacho et al. (2018) — *Next-Generation Machine Learning for Biological Networks*, Cell

### Session 6 — Genomic Surveillance & Taxonomic Profiling
- Mäklin et al. (2021) — *Bacterial genomic epidemiology with mixed samples*, Microbial Genomics
- Ye et al. (2019) — *Benchmarking Metagenomics Tools for Taxonomic Classification*, Cell
