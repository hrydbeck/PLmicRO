# Zotero Group Library

We use a shared **Zotero group library** to collect and organise all papers for the journal club. Joining the group gives you access to the full collection — including PDFs, notes, and tags — and lets you add papers yourself.

---

## Join the Group

1. **Create a free Zotero account** at [zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org/user/register) (if you don't already have one).
2. **Open our group page:**
   👉 [**PLmicRO Journal Club on Zotero**](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6445107)
3. Click **"Join"** (or **"Apply for Membership"** if the group requires approval).
4. Once you're a member, the group library will appear automatically in the Zotero desktop app and on the Zotero web interface.

---

## Install Zotero

Zotero is available for installation on computers on the hospital network. Once installed, sign in with your Zotero account and the group library will sync automatically.

---

## Share a Paper via Zotero

Once you're a member of the group, you can add papers directly:

### From the browser (easiest)
1. Navigate to the paper on the publisher's website (e.g. PubMed, journal page).
2. Click the **Zotero Connector** icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Choose the **PLmicRO Journal Club** group library as the destination.
4. The paper (with metadata and often the PDF) is saved and shared with everyone.

### From the desktop app
1. Open Zotero and expand the **PLmicRO Journal Club** group in the left panel.
2. Drag a paper into the appropriate collection, or use **File → Add by Identifier** and paste a DOI.

### From the web library
1. Go to [our Zotero group library](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6445107/library).
2. Click the green **➕** button to add a new item manually or by identifier (DOI, ISBN, PMID).

---

## Tips

- **Use DOIs** when adding papers — Zotero will fetch full metadata automatically.
- **Add tags** like the session number or topic to help organise papers.
- Papers added to the group library are visible to all members immediately after syncing.
- You can also annotate PDFs in Zotero and your highlights will be shared with the group.
