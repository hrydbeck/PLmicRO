## Series 2: AI and Precision Medicine

### Session 7 — Foundations of AI in Precision Medicine

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Rajkomar A, Dean J, Kohane I — *Machine Learning in Medicine* | N Engl J Med | 2019 |
| Topol EJ — *High-performance medicine: the convergence of human and artificial intelligence* | Nature Medicine | 2019 |

**Why these papers?** Two landmark overviews framing how ML is reshaping clinical practice — from diagnostics to treatment selection.

---

### Session 8 — Genomic Medicine & Variant Interpretation

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Poplin R et al. — *A universal SNP and small-indel variant caller using deep neural networks* | Nature Biotechnology | 2018 |
| Sundaram L et al. — *Predicting the clinical impact of human mutation with deep neural networks* | Nature Genetics | 2018 |

**Why these papers?** DeepVariant revolutionised variant calling with CNNs; PrimateAI tackles variant pathogenicity prediction.

---

### Session 9 — Clinical Decision Support & Diagnostics

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Esteva A et al. — *Dermatologist-level classification of skin cancer with deep neural networks* | Nature | 2017 |
| Tomašev N et al. — *A clinically applicable approach to continuous prediction of future acute kidney injury* | Nature | 2019 |

**Why these papers?** Two flagship studies showing AI matching specialist performance — skin cancer classification and AKI prediction from EHR time-series.

### Session 10 — Drug Discovery & Repurposing

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Stokes JH et al. — *A deep learning approach to antibiotic discovery* | Cell | 2020 |
| Vamathevan J et al. — *Applications of machine learning in drug discovery and development* | Nature Reviews Drug Discovery | 2019 |

**Why these papers?** Stokes et al. used graph neural networks to discover a novel antibiotic (halicin) — directly relevant to clinical microbiology.

### Session 11 — Patient Stratification & Biomarker Discovery

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Swanson K et al. — *From patterns to patients: Advances in clinical machine learning for cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment* | Cell | 2023 |

**Why this paper?** Covers multi-omics integration, patient subtyping, and treatment response prediction using modern ML techniques.

### Session 12 — Clinical NLP, LLMs & Future Directions

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Singhal K et al. — *Large language models encode clinical knowledge* | Nature | 2023 |
