# Reading List

Six sessions covering AI and machine learning in clinical microbiology, from foundational concepts to applied genomic surveillance.

---

## Session 1 — Intro to ML for Biologists

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Greener et al. — *A guide to machine learning for biologists* | Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology | 2022 |

**Why this paper?** Accessible overview of model types, evaluation, and pitfalls — builds shared vocabulary for the group.

---

## Session 2 — Benchmarking & Comparing AI Models

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Topçuoğlu et al. — *A Framework for Effective Application of Machine Learning to Microbiome-Based Classification Problems* | mBio | 2020 |

**Why this paper?** Practical guide to proper cross-validation, metric selection, and avoiding data leakage.

---

## Session 3 — AI in Clinical Microbiology (Reviews)

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Smith et al. — *Machine Learning and Deep Learning in Clinical Microbiology: A Review* | Clinical Microbiology Reviews | 2023 |
| Peiffer-Smadja et al. — *Machine learning for clinical decision support in infectious diseases* | Clinical Microbiology and Infection | 2020 |

**Why these papers?** Comprehensive review covering pathogen ID, AMR prediction, outbreak detection, and practical clinical applications.

---

## Session 4 — MALDI-TOF + ML

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Weis et al. — *Machine learning for microbial identification and AST on MALDI-TOF mass spectra* | Clinical Microbiology and Infection | 2020 |

**Why this paper?** Concrete clinical micro workflow that many labs can relate to.

---

## Session 5 — Deep Learning in Genomics

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Eraslan et al. — *Deep learning: new computational modelling techniques for genomics* | Nature Reviews Genetics | 2019 |
| Camacho et al. — *Next-Generation Machine Learning for Biological Networks* | Cell | 2018 |

**Why these papers?** CNNs, RNNs, autoencoders in a genomics context, plus supervised/unsupervised/reinforcement learning applied to biological networks.

---

## Session 6 — Genomic Surveillance & Taxonomic Profiling

| Paper | Journal | Year |
|-------|---------|------|
| Mäklin et al. — *Bacterial genomic epidemiology with mixed samples* | Microbial Genomics | 2021 |
| Ye et al. — *Benchmarking Metagenomics Tools for Taxonomic Classification* | Cell | 2019 |

**Why these papers?** Directly relevant to surveillance workflows (MIMOSA, nf-core/taxprofiler).
